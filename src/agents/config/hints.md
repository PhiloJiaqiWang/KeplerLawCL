# Hint Database

## Kepler First Law

- A circle has one center. Every point on the circle is the same distance from the center.
- An ellipse has two foci. The sum of the distances from any point on the ellipse to the two foci remains constant.
- Distance measurements can provide evidence about the shape of an orbit.
- Compare distances from multiple orbit points.


## Kepler Second Law

- Compare equal time intervals at different orbit locations.
- Check whether swept areas stay similar even when speed changes.
- Separate speed evidence from swept-area evidence before interpreting.


## Kepler Third Law

- Compare orbital period and semi-major axis across multiple orbits.
- Look for whether larger orbits also have longer periods.
- Use pairs of period and axis measurements from the same orbit.
